# NCE 3 Missing Image Prompts (Ghibli Style)

Use these prompts with an image generation tool (like Midjourney v6, DALL-E 3, or Stable Diffusion) to create illustrations for the missing lessons.

**Style Guide:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background, watercolor textures, warm lighting, nostalgic atmosphere.

---

## Lesson 51: Predicting the Future
**Scene:** A contrast between a massive 1970s mainframe computer room and a modern handheld device.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A split composition or blended scene. On one side, a 1970s computer room with a massive mainframe machine filling the wall and a technician in a white coat working on it. On the other side, a bright, modern living room with a person relaxing on a sofa holding a small, sleek tablet. Warm colors, nostalgic yet futuristic atmosphere, detailed machinery and cozy furniture.

## Lesson 52: Mud is Mud
**Scene:** Harry's study with the curious bottle of mud.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A cozy, cluttered study room with wooden bookshelves filled with books. On a wooden desk, a curiously-shaped green glass bottle filled with thick greyish mud is displayed prominently. A man (Harry) stands proudly next to it, smiling. Warm amber lighting, detailed textures of old books, wood grain, and the mysterious bottle.

## Lesson 53: In the Public Interest
**Scene:** The Ombudsman in his office reading a letter.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A kind-looking elderly official (the Ombudsman) sitting at a large wooden desk piled high with stacks of letters and documents in a warm, wood-paneled office. He is holding one letter and reading it carefully. Sunlight streams through a window, illuminating floating dust motes. A sense of justice, diligence, and care.

## Lesson 54: Instinct or Cleverness
**Scene:** The writer observing ants on a peach tree in a garden.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A lush, green garden with a large peach tree growing against a warm red brick wall. A man is crouching down, intently observing a line of ants crawling up the tree trunk. A band of sticky tape is wrapped around the base of the tree. Summer atmosphere, dappled sunlight through the leaves, vibrant greens and earthy tones.

## Lesson 55: From the Earth: Greetings
**Scene:** A view of space and a telescope searching for life.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A dreamy, majestic view of outer space with a glowing planet and distant stars. In the foreground, a large, intricate space telescope (like Hubble) floats in orbit. A sense of wonder and vastness. Deep blues, purples, and bright starlight. whimsical and beautiful representation of the cosmos.

## Lesson 56: Our Neighbour, the River
**Scene:** A family floating on a raft during a flood.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A flooded meadow landscape where the water is calm but covers the fields. A makeshift wooden raft floats on the water carrying a family (a man, woman, and children), a dog, a cat, and a birdcage. They look worried but safe. Green trees and farmhouses are partially submerged in the background. Dramatic but soft lighting, water reflections.

## Lesson 57: Back in the Old Country
**Scene:** The narrator looking at a map near a man-made lake.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A young man standing beside a parked vintage car on a quiet country road, looking confusedly at a map in his hands. In front of him is a large, calm lake where a valley and village should be. Green hills in the distance. A man on horseback is approaching from the horizon. Melancholic, peaceful, and beautiful atmosphere.

## Lesson 58: A Spot of Bother
**Scene:** The old lady discovering her ransacked flat.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. An elderly lady standing in the doorway of her living room, looking shocked and upset. The room is in chaos, with clothes, papers, and books scattered everywhere from drawers and shelves. Sunlight illuminates the messy room. A sense of disarray in a once-cozy home.

## Lesson 59: Collecting
**Scene:** A collector in a room full of treasures.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A cluttered but organized room filled with various collectibles on shelves and tables: antique clocks, rare books, glass bottles, and model cars. A person is sitting at a desk, carefully examining a small object or stamp with a magnifying glass. Warm, golden lighting, a feeling of contentment and fascination.

## Lesson 60: Too Early and Too Late
**Scene:** A girl crying at a train station as the train leaves.
**Prompt:**
> Ghibli Studio style, anime/manga, vibrant, painterly, detailed background. A vintage train station platform. A young girl with a suitcase stands crying, watching a steam train depart in the distance. A station master in a uniform stands nearby, looking sympathetic but firm. Nostalgic railway setting, steam, and soft afternoon light.
